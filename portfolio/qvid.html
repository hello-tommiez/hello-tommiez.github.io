QVID - AI Video Generator

An AI platform for creating and editing videos, built on one idea: “No skill, no problem.” At OceanLabs, I was the Product Designer on QVID - from researching video and voice AI with our BA, to building the design system and UI, to shipping it with the dev team.

Company
OceanLabs
Role
Product Designer
Timeline
Oct 2025 – Sep 2026
Tools
Figma, Antigravity, v0

The brief

OceanLabs set out to build QVID, a web platform where anyone can turn an idea into a finished video - script, voiceover, visuals and edits - without any editing skills. The audience was creators, marketers and small businesses who need short-form video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts but find traditional editors too hard or too slow. Working with a BA, I researched the AI video and sound models available and analysed who our users were and what they needed.

Challenge

At the time, AI models could only generate clips up to 3 minutes long. Anything longer had to be stitched together from several generations, so characters, scenes and voices could easily drift out of sync from one clip to the next. The hardest part was designing the prompts that chain each screen and generation step together - and building checks into every stage of the flow, so consistency was verified along the way instead of discovered at the end.

Process

Together with the BA, I benchmarked video and voice AI models and turned our user analysis into one guided flow: start from an idea, let QVID generate the video, then refine and export. From there I built QVID’s design system and designed the landing page and the web app - dashboard, voice and asset libraries, and a timeline editor for text, media, audio and captions. To move fast, I used AI tools like Antigravity and v0 to prototype, and worked closely with developers through handoff and QA to get the product to users.

Outcome

QVID reached real users with a complete journey from idea to an export-ready video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. The product is no longer active, but it opened up a new domain for me - generative video and audio and changed how I work: bringing AI into my process to build fast, and using it to vibe-code alongside the team.
