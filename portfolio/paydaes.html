The brief

Replace spreadsheets, paper forms and email approvals with one HR platform.

HR admins Managers Employees
Challenge

5 agencies

Every payroll run must satisfy EPF, SOCSO, EIS, LHDN and Zakat, each with its own rates, forms and deadlines. I designed the foundation tables first so one payroll run could compute and file them all.
